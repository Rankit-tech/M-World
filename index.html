Questions, answered

Is this radio free?

Yes. It plays in your browser on phone or desktop, with no sign-up.

What kind of music plays here?

Old Hindi film songs and the sounds around them, grouped into stations by mood: the barber shop, the highway, dad's cassette shelf, the building site.

How do I change the station?

Tap a station name at the top, or use Prev and Next on the radio.

What does the Rain button do?

It starts a rainy-day effect with falling rain on the screen and the sound of a monsoon shower. Tap it again to stop.

How can I support the radio?

Tap Support at the top. Your support keeps the radio free.
