    Songs

    Songs are ranked by listeners: the most listened song is #1 and plays first on the site. A listen counts when someone stays for 20 seconds, and a full play counts double. New songs start at the bottom and move up as people listen. Use the three dots on a song to move it to any position (#1, #2...), edit or delete it. A moved song stays pinned there until you unpin it.

    Ranking is not set up yet. Run supabase/update-v2.sql in the Supabase SQL Editor.

    Wallpapers

    Change the background photo of each theme. Photos are shrunk automatically to save space. Use a wide photo (landscape). "Reset" brings back the original photo.

    Assistant

    The assistant reads your listening numbers and tells you which songs are popular, which ones people skip, and what to do next. It is a built-in rule-based helper, not an online AI service, so it costs nothing and needs no key.

    Chat moderation

    Latest 50 messages. Every visitor has a fixed ID (like MW-4F2A91) and cannot change it. Use the three dots to delete one message or everything from one ID.
