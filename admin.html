    Upload songs

    Add one or many songs. Each song is one card: you can rename it, and add its own thumbnail and background video, all before uploading.

    mp3, m4a, ogg or wav, up to 15 MB each. Thumbnail: jpg, png or webp. Video: mp4 or webm, up to 50 MB (compress to 720p first).

    Songs admin v12

    Songs are ranked by listeners: the most listened song is #1 and plays first on the site. A listen counts when someone stays for 20 seconds, and a full play counts double. New songs start at the bottom and move up as people listen. Use the three dots on a song to move it to any position (#1, #2...), edit or delete it. A moved song stays pinned there until you unpin it.

    Ranking is not set up yet. Run supabase/update-v2.sql in the Supabase SQL Editor.

    Wallpapers and visibility

    Use the switch to make a whole theme public (green) or private (grey). A private theme is hidden from visitors. You still see it while signed in as admin; to see the site like a visitor, open it with ?visitor at the end of the address. Change the background photo of each theme. Photos are shrunk automatically to save space. Use a wide photo (landscape). "Reset" brings back the original photo.

    Song requests

    What listeners asked for. Upload the song (only if you own it or it is licensed) and press Done, or press Reject. Run supabase/update-v9.sql once if this stays empty with an error.

    Insights

    What listeners actually play, and what to do next. The site plays songs in a fresh random order for every visitor, so these numbers are for you to decide which songs to add or remove. Built in, free, no key needed.
